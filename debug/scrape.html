Website Accessibility

  1. San Francisco Recreation and Parks is committed to ensuring that our digital services are accessible to all users, including people with disabilities.

    To help us respond as effectively as possible, please include:

    • The web page address (URL) where you encountered the issue
    • A description of the problem
